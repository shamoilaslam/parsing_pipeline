Section 20 is attracted when a person not already enjoying water supply requires canal water for his land and such supply is to be conveyed through already existing watercourse, or when any person obtained land in which water is not previously sanctioned and he wants to get it included in the warabandi of any outlet. But if land already stands included in the Chakbandi and CCA of any outlet the owner of such land can get his land included in the warabandi under section 68. Because the water of such land is already flowing in the watercourse, therefore, section20 is attracted onlyfor a fresh supply for any other land which is not already in C.C.A.. or is not in Chakbandi of such outlet.
